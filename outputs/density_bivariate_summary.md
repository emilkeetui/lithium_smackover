# HUC12 Li concentration × population density — class counts

Li breaks (mg/L): ['< 100', '100–200', '≥ 200']. Population-density breaks (people/km²): ['< 5', '5–25', '25–621']; classes are half-open, lower-inclusive. Density is the LandScan 2024 population of the whole HUC12 divided by its equal-area (Albers) area. Each HUC12's Li value is the median or mean of every valid 2 km cell overlapping it (equal weight per cell).
